# Myanmar Maritime Careers

Create a professional, modern one-page corporate website for GHPs Management Co., Ltd., a Myanmar-based hospitality management and human resources company establishing a Cruise Crew Career Development & Recruitment division.

The website should communicate credibility, transparency, international hospitality experience and responsible career development.

Target audiences are international cruise lines, global crewing companies, maritime employers, hospitality training institutions and young Myanmar professionals interested in legitimate careers at sea.

Visual style: sophisticated maritime hospitality, deep navy and white with restrained gold accents, professional photography, clean typography, generous spacing, premium but trustworthy rather than flashy.

Sections: Home, About GHPs, Founder, Cruise Crew Career Development, Life at Sea, Responsible Recruitment, Training & Skills Development, For International Employers, Training Partners, Crew Development Updates, Candidate Registration, Contact.

The founder has more than 20 years of cruise and hotel experience, beginning in international cruise operations in 1998 and later working with Louis Cruises, Paradise Cruises and TUI Cruises/Mein Schiff, including an HR Assistant Manager role.

The company should NOT claim to guarantee employment and should NOT claim to be a licensed seafarer recruitment agency until the relevant Myanmar regulatory authorization has been obtained.

Emphasize candidate safety, professional skills, English communication, multicultural awareness, shipboard conduct, social responsibility, document verification and transparent recruitment.

Include strong calls to action for “International Recruitment Partners” and “Candidate Registration”.

Make the site fully mobile responsive and suitable for future updates about screening events, training sessions, recruitment activities and partnerships.
